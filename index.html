My art

Verify first. Then improvise.

Credible guests, everyday questions and nothing scripted. That is what keeps every episode honest, spontaneous and real.

01

Before an episode, Sagar visits his guests at their clinics, hospitals and workplaces.

02

Check the claims first-hand

He tests what guests say for himself, from trying Agnikarma therapy to seeing their work up close.

03

No script. Pure conversation.

Every interview is improvised. He follows the story wherever it goes and asks what the audience would ask.

The story
